# Early builders

People who built, tested, broke or explained tern while it was young. This list is the whole reward: there is no token, no points, no allocation and no promise of one, now or later.

To be added, open a pull request that adds one line: your name or handle, and a link to what you did (an example app, a bug report that was fixed, a ranking module, a data node, a write-up, a translation). A maintainer merges it once the thing exists.
